It was goodbye.

When the Sky Lost Its Rainbow

People say I should move on.

And I…
I am still the author who gave it everything.

But this time, I won’t try to fill it again.
Not because I don’t feel anymore— but because I finally learned something:

Depth doesn’t come from writing alone… it comes from being read, understood, and felt.

And maybe one day, I’ll find a page that doesn’t stay empty.
A page that writes back. A page that holds my words the way I hold them.

But until then—
you will remain the page I couldn’t finish.
And I will remain the author who learned that not every story is meant to be written…
even if it was felt deeply.












I lost it all because of you— and still, you left me in ways I never thought I’d see.





















Where can I find you once more— in the silence, or in my memories? Do I search the roads we never walked, or the dreams we left unfinished? Because no matter where I look… it’s only you I’m trying to find.
